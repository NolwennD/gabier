## New in 0.7.2

- **Every line of the daemon log begins with its time.** The log, `<socket>.log` next to the socket of the daemon, now
  gives each line the local time of the machine with its offset, to the second (`2026-11-26T09:43:12+01:00 ...`): the lines
  of the daemon, the statuses of jdtls and what jdtls writes on its standard error, which the log did not date before.
  The time between two lines, such as the start of jdtls and its `ServiceReady`, can be read from the log alone. See
  `gabier help start` and `docs/daemon.md`.
- **A warning of a library of the daemon is one line in the log.** What LSP4J writes with `java.util.logging` (for a message
  of jdtls it does not know) was two lines, the second with no time; it is now one dated line. When the record carries an
  exception, the line gives its type and message and not its stack trace.
- No command, option or refusal changes.
